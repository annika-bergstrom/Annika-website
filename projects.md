  <summary class="main-summary">Hannah Holborn Gray Fellowship</summary>
 <br> 
  <p>I completed a Hannah Holborn Gray Fellowship in the summer before my senior year, advised by Professor Miriam Kamil of the Classics department and Professor Mary Alcaro of the English department. Over the course of my research, I focused on a comparative analysis of Ovid's "Iphis and Ianthe" to the Old French "Yde et Olive". I had a specific intrest in gender differentation between and across the two texts, and in tracing the theme of transformation and translation across and between them. The final paper I produced on this research was titled "'I know it when I see it': Constructing Manhood and Gender Transformation in Iphis and Ianthe and Yde et Olive". </p>


  </details>
